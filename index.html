About

The path that got me here

I grew up in Mumbai and studied electrical engineering there, then left India at 21 for a master's in Energy Science and Technology at ETH Zürich. From there: a PhD in Erlangen-Nürnberg built on real-world data from the energy transition, a research stint at UCL in London, and now a role at Siemens Energy in Berlin, where I still work with universities on research and data projects.

Along the way I've taught university courses, supervised eleven bachelor's and master's students through their thesis projects, and helped students from outside Europe through their own move here. This site is that experience, made available to students in India who are earlier on the same road I was on.

Services

How I can help

Most of what I offer falls into five areas. Most people come to me for one or two of these, not all five.

Choosing a program and a country

Bachelor's, master's, or PhD, and where — we work through your goals, budget, and what you want daily life to look like, not just rankings.

  • Shortlist programs that actually fit your background and interests
  • Compare Germany, Switzerland, and the UK on cost, work rights, and job prospects
  • Talk through which cities and program sizes suit different personalities

Applications that get read

Statement of purpose, CV, recommendation letters, and the parts of the process Germany and Switzerland don't explain clearly.

  • Honest feedback on your SOP and CV, not just proofreading
  • A realistic read on where you're competitive and where you're reaching
  • Country-by-country timelines and document checklists

Research mentoring and academic writing

If your program involves a thesis, this is where I can add something most consultants can't: formal research training.

  • Narrowing a research proposal into something you can actually finish
  • Choosing and defending a methodology
  • A critical, questioning sounding board — the kind a busy supervisor rarely has time to be
  • Structure and clarity in academic writing, not ghostwriting

Living in Germany, Switzerland, or the UK

I've lived in Zürich, Erlangen/Nuremberg, London, and now Berlin. This is the part no brochure explains.

  • Visa and residence permit basics from someone who's been through them
  • Housing, banking, and health insurance in the first weeks
  • A candid conversation about homesickness, isolation, and what actually helps
